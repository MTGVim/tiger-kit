# Drive ledger

`.tigerkit/drive.md` is the only Markdown lifecycle ledger for one Drive run.
Atomically replace the current task in the repo/worktree-local file and reread it.
Do not archive old tasks or create pointers, cursors, schedulers, or global state.
Artifact presence grants no authority.

Keep compact evidence for:

- task/source identity, R/AC, and source anchors;
- scope, exclusions, frozen source literals, and durable prior art;
- controller-resolved assumptions/ambiguities with basis and material alternatives;
- explicit user decisions and the approved plan snapshot;
- unit dependency graph, waves, dispatch tier or `host-default`, and ownership;
- test/browser obligations and non-sensitive auth mode;
- per-unit candidate, changed paths, verifier/gap verdict, corrections, and commit;
- aggregate verification, terminal status, and non-success recovery evidence.

Nested workers, reviewers, and verifiers write no competing Markdown ledger.
Binary evidence may live in a referenced run-owned directory. Do not store raw
transcripts, chain of thought, full logs/diffs, provider/model names, passwords,
tokens, cookies, OTPs, private identity, or any other secret value.
