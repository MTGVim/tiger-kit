# Independent Content Review

Ordinary chat and feedback on a learner's answer
do not start this artifact workflow.

Use a separate reviewer with fresh context: the final saved artifact and its exact version,
the audience's confirmed knowledge and labeled assumptions, understanding/action goal, constraints (including
explicit voice, register, formatting requirements and relevant user examples) and necessary
sources with version bindings. Omit the author's intended explanation, self-assessment and
expected verdict. The reviewer reads what the artifact actually teaches, including figures,
examples, question inputs and revealed answers. For HTML, skip shared CSS/JS/export payloads
unless needed for the content, retaining figure/state logic and evidence anchors.

Challenge hidden premises, missing definitions or causal steps, unintroduced types, and
unexplained spatial positions/order/routes. Can this reader understand the conclusion or
perform the first action using the taught material? Do examples and exercises provide the
inputs that decision needs? For a visual exercise, can a reader match each name or symbol
in the question and solution to a visibly identified figure and location, rather than relying
on hidden SVG metadata or an answer-only label? For an ordered or spatial mechanism, can the
reader track each relevant actor, start state, intermediate transition and result without
inventing the missing positions? If an interactive sequence is used, does the actual drawing
change with the described step, rather than only the caption? A static drawing remains valid
when it explains the goal without hidden intermediate states. Author intent,
section/table/figure counts and render success cannot supply missing teaching or establish reader mastery.

Each blocking finding names its location, missing information, the consequence for the
reader's goal and minimum correction. Prioritize those failures over incidental stylistic preference, but explicitly requested
voice or presentation constraints are acceptance requirements. Check whether introductions
and transitions guide the reader through what to inspect or try when that voice is requested;
do not require all factual definitions to become invitations or questions. A valid review may
find no blockers. The reviewer is read-only and returns a verdict plus
findings, without extra reviewers or a required report file.

Record the mandatory requirements actually inspected with the verdict; a `Pass` for factual
content does not imply an unreviewed voice, visible-label or sequence requirement passed.
Repair the owning content and regenerate before re-reviewing the current saved version.
If independent review or essential evidence is unavailable, report `Unverifiable` and the
pending requirement; do not substitute self-review or claim completed production.
