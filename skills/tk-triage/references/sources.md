# Origin and design choices

Source: Matt Pocock, `mattpocock/skills`,
`skills/engineering/triage/SKILL.md` (MIT, inspected 2026-10-08).
Keep issue-first evidence, missing-info collection, agent-ready briefs, and
separation of human action. Adapt: optional tracker-neutral project policy
with unstructured strings and explicit settings onboarding, no-op/report-first
behavior, and mandatory target-bound remote authorization. Omit rigid global
labels/state machine, automatic publication, dependencies on project plugins,
or an orchestration runtime.
